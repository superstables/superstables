description: Get started with Superstables
---

Read the Superstables skill and help the owner get started. Explain that this release uses test tokens and that the owner approves a budget or a single purchase in their wallet. In a chat app (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot), don't offer a budget or approval on the owner's machine: offer a single purchase the owner approves on superstables.com, and say that a budget needs Claude Code, Codex or another agent that can run commands. Otherwise, ask whether they want a budget or a single purchase and whether to approve on superstables.com or on their machine, unless they have already said. Follow the skill for the chosen route. If a budget amount is missing, ask for it before starting a grant. Never approve or sign for the owner.
